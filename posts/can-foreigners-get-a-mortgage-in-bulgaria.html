Can Foreigners Get a Mortgage in Bulgaria?

midnight_tide
04.06.2026 • Read time: 5 min

Bulgaria has long been a magnet for international investors and lifestyle seekers, drawn by its stunning Black Sea coastline, affordable mountain resorts, and a cost of living that remains among the lowest in the European Union. If you are considering investing in this Balkan gem, you may find yourself asking: Can foreigners get a mortgage in Bulgaria? The short answer is yes, but the process is significantly more complex than for local citizens.

Understanding the Mortgage Landscape for Non-Residents

While Bulgarian banks are generally open to lending to foreigners, they operate under strict risk-assessment protocols. Obtaining a mortgage as a non-resident is not a standard retail banking product; it is a specialized service that often requires personal negotiation with bank officers. The accessibility of these loans depends heavily on your country of origin, your residency status in Bulgaria, and the nature of the property you intend to purchase.

Before diving into the financial requirements, it is essential to understand the legal framework. If you are in the early stages of your search, it is highly recommended to read our comprehensive guide on how to buy property in Bulgaria as a foreigner to ensure you have the correct documentation and legal standing to proceed with a purchase.

Key Eligibility Requirements for Foreign Borrowers

Bulgarian banks typically categorize foreign applicants into two groups: EU/EEA citizens and non-EU citizens. While EU citizens often enjoy a smoother path, both groups must demonstrate proof of stable income and creditworthiness. Common requirements include:

  • Proof of Income: Banks will require verified income statements, usually translated into Bulgarian by a sworn translator.
  • Residency Status: Holding a long-term or permanent residency permit in Bulgaria significantly increases your chances of approval.
  • Credit History: While Bulgaria does not have a centralized international credit database, banks may request credit reports from your home country.
  • Down Payment: Foreigners are typically expected to provide a higher down payment compared to locals, often ranging between 30% and 50% of the property's appraised value.

The Property Appraisal and Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio

It is crucial to note that banks do not base the loan amount on the purchase price stated in the preliminary contract. Instead, they rely on an independent property appraisal. The bank-appointed valuer will determine the "market value," and the bank will base its LTV ratio on that lower figure. Financial Implications and Hidden Costs

Securing a mortgage is only one part of the financial puzzle. Many foreign buyers underestimate the secondary expenses associated with property ownership in Bulgaria. Beyond the mortgage interest and principal payments, you must account for notary fees, local taxes, insurance, and maintenance costs. You can find a detailed breakdown of these expenses in our article on the hidden costs of buying property in Bulgaria explained.

Steps to Increase Your Chances of Approval

  1. Open a Local Bank Account: Establish a relationship with a Bulgarian bank early in the process.
  2. Hire a Local Lawyer: A legal expert can help navigate the complex bureaucracy and ensure your loan application is professionally presented.
  3. Prepare Your Documentation: Ensure all financial documents are apostilled or legalized as required by Bulgarian law.
  4. Consider a Mortgage Broker: Specialized brokers in Bulgaria often have direct contacts with bank managers who are authorized to approve loans for non-residents.

Conclusion

Getting a mortgage as a foreigner in Bulgaria is entirely possible, though it requires patience and preparation. By understanding the local banking culture, maintaining clean financial records, and being prepared for a higher initial capital outlay, you can successfully leverage Bulgarian banking products to secure your dream property. Always conduct thorough due diligence and consult with local professionals to ensure your investment is both secure and financially sound.
