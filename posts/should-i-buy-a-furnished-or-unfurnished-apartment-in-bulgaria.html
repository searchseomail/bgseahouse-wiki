Should I Buy a Furnished or Unfurnished Apartment in Bulgaria?

flora_fable
30.07.2026 • Read time: 5 min

Deciding between a furnished and an unfurnished apartment in Bulgaria is one of the most critical choices you will make during your property search. Whether you are looking for a holiday home on the Black Sea coast, a city apartment in Sofia, or a mountain retreat in Bansko, the state of the property will significantly impact your budget, timeline, and overall experience as a homeowner.

The Case for Buying a Furnished Apartment

For many international buyers, purchasing a furnished apartment is the "turnkey" solution. It is particularly popular in resort areas like Sunny Beach or Golden Sands, where developers or previous owners have already equipped the units for short-term rentals.

  • Immediate Occupancy: You can literally walk in with a suitcase and start living there the same day.
  • Rental Potential: If your goal is to generate income, a fully equipped unit is ready for guests immediately, saving you months of interior design and logistics.
  • Budget Transparency: While the purchase price might be higher, you avoid the hidden costs of shipping furniture, assembly, and appliance installation.

However, it is vital to be cautious. Before finalizing your purchase, ensure you understand common real estate scams in Bulgaria and how to avoid them, especially regarding the condition of appliances hidden behind "move-in ready" marketing.

The Case for Buying an Unfurnished Apartment

Buying an unfurnished apartment—or a "BDS" (Bulgarian State Standard) property—offers a blank canvas. This is often the preferred route for those looking to relocate permanently or those who want to ensure the quality of every piece of furniture in their home.

  • Personalization: You have total control over the aesthetic, quality, and layout of your living space.
  • Long-term Value: By choosing your own materials and furniture, you ensure better quality control, which can be beneficial if you are planning how to get residency in Bulgaria by buying property and intend to stay for many years.
  • Lower Entry Price: Unfurnished units are generally cheaper, leaving you more liquid capital to renovate according to your own standards.

Comparison at a Glance

Feature Furnished Unfurnished
Initial Investment Higher Lower

Customization Limited Unlimited
Maintenance Often higher complexity Easier to manage early on

Hidden Considerations: Maintenance and Quality

Regardless of your choice, you must account for the ongoing expenses associated with keeping your property in top condition. Many buyers underestimate the climate-specific requirements in Bulgaria, such as humidity management in coastal areas or heating efficiency in mountain regions. Understanding the real cost of property maintenance in Bulgaria is essential to avoid surprises after you have settled in.

Quality of Furniture in Resorts

If you are looking at resorts, be aware that "furnished" can range from high-end designer furniture to budget-grade pieces that may wear out within two or three seasons. Always request a detailed inventory list (an "opis") as part of your preliminary contract. If the furniture is of poor quality, you may find yourself paying for repairs or replacements sooner than expected, which can quickly erase any savings you thought you made by buying a furnished unit.

The "BDS" Standard Explained

In Bulgaria, you will frequently see the term "BDS." This means the apartment is delivered with plastered walls and a screeded floor, but without flooring, interior doors, or light fixtures. While this sounds daunting, it is the standard for new builds. It gives you the advantage of installing modern wiring and plumbing fixtures that meet your exact specifications, rather than relying on the developer's often cost-cut choices.

Final Verdict

If you are a vacation home buyer who wants zero hassle, a furnished apartment is usually the better path. If you are an investor or a long-term resident looking for quality and personalization, buying an unfurnished apartment allows you to curate your environment and ensure the long-term integrity of your investment.
