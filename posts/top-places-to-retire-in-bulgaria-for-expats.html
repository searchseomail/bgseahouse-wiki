Top Places to Retire in Bulgaria for Expats

copper_fox
18.06.2026 • Read time: 8 min

Bulgaria has emerged as a premier destination for retirees seeking an affordable, culturally rich, and scenic lifestyle in the European Union. With its mild climate, low cost of living, and breathtaking landscapes—ranging from the rugged Balkan Mountains to the golden beaches of the Black Sea—it is no wonder that the expat community continues to grow. If you are considering making the move, understanding the logistics is crucial, including how to buy property in Bulgaria as a foreigner, which is a surprisingly straightforward process compared to other Western European nations.

Why Bulgaria is Perfect for Retirees

Retiring in Bulgaria offers a unique blend of modern convenience and traditional charm. The country boasts one of the lowest tax rates in the EU, making your pension go much further. Furthermore, the healthcare system is accessible, and the local lifestyle encourages a slower, healthier pace of living. Before you finalize your move, it is wise to research understanding property taxes in Bulgaria to ensure your budget is perfectly aligned with your long-term plans.

Top Regions for Expats

1. The Black Sea Coast: Varna and Burgas

For those who love the sea, the coastal cities are the top choice. Varna, often called the "Sea Capital," offers a vibrant cultural scene, beautiful parks, and international connectivity. Burgas, on the other hand, is known for its relaxed atmosphere and proximity to the stunning seaside town of Sozopol.

  • Pros: Excellent climate, beach access, active expat communities.
  • Cons: Can get crowded and noisy during the peak summer tourist season.

2. Veliko Tarnovo: The Historical Heart

Nestled in the hills of central Bulgaria, Veliko Tarnovo is a favorite among expats who prefer history and mountain views over the coast. It is a university city with a youthful energy, yet it offers a quiet, dignified lifestyle for retirees. The architecture is stunning, and the cost of living remains significantly lower than in the capital.

3. Bansko: The Mountain Haven

While famous for its world-class ski resort, Bansko has transformed into a year-round hub for expats. It offers fresh mountain air, thermal springs, and an incredibly low cost of living. It is ideal for those who enjoy hiking, nature, and a tight-knit community of international residents.

Comparison of Living Costs and Lifestyle

Choosing the right location depends on your personal preferences for climate and community. Below is a quick comparison of the top regions:

Location Primary Appeal Vibe
Varna Seafront Living Urban & Energetic
Veliko Tarnovo Culture & History Traditional & Scenic
Bansko Nature & Mountains Relaxed & Budget-friendly

Essential Tips for a Smooth Transition

Moving abroad is a significant life change. Beyond finding the right house, you should be aware of the hidden costs of buying property in Bulgaria, such as notary fees, local taxes, and ongoing maintenance. Always visit your potential new home during the "off-season" to see what life is like when the tourists have left, and consider renting for a few months before committing to a purchase.

Bulgaria offers a welcoming environment for retirees, providing a high quality of life at a fraction of the cost found elsewhere in Europe. By doing your research and connecting with local expat groups, you can successfully navigate your relocation and enjoy your golden years in this beautiful Balkan gem.
