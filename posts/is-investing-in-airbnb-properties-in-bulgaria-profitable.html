Is Investing in Airbnb Properties in Bulgaria Profitable?

lunar_logic
30.06.2026 • Read time: 8 min

Bulgaria has emerged as one of Europe’s most compelling real estate markets for investors seeking high rental yields and low entry costs. Whether you are looking at the sun-drenched Black Sea coast or the snow-capped peaks of Bansko, the short-term rental market via platforms like Airbnb has transformed how property owners monetize their assets. But is investing in Airbnb properties in Bulgaria truly profitable in the current economic climate?

The Economic Appeal of the Bulgarian Rental Market

The primary driver behind the profitability of Bulgarian Airbnb properties is the favorable ratio between purchase price and potential rental income. Compared to Western European hubs like Paris, London, or even neighboring Greece, property prices in Bulgaria remain highly competitive. This allows investors to achieve a faster return on investment (ROI), often seeing annual yields ranging between 5% and 10% in high-demand areas.

Key Factors Influencing Profitability

  • Seasonality: Bulgaria offers year-round potential. Coastal resorts thrive from June to September, while mountain ski resorts see high occupancy from December to March.
  • Low Taxation: Bulgaria boasts a flat 10% corporate and personal income tax rate, which significantly boosts net profit margins compared to other EU jurisdictions.
  • Cost of Living: Low maintenance and utility costs mean that a larger portion of your Airbnb revenue stays in your pocket rather than going toward overheads.

Strategic Locations for Maximum Yields

Not all properties are created equal. Your geographic choice is the single most important factor in determining the success of your Airbnb venture.

Region Primary Season Target Audience
Sunny Beach / Varna Summer Families, party-goers, digital nomads
Bansko / Borovets Winter Skiers, snowboarders, remote workers
Sofia Year-round Business travelers, city-break tourists

While urban and resort areas are the standard choice, some investors look toward the countryside for unique "off-the-beaten-path" experiences. However, if you are considering this route, ensure you know what to check before buying rural property in Bulgaria, as infrastructure and accessibility can vary significantly from the major hubs.

Operational Challenges and Management

Profitability is not just about the purchase price; it is about the operational efficiency of your listing. Managing an Airbnb remotely can be a logistical nightmare, especially if you do not speak the local language or understand Bulgarian rental regulations. Many successful investors choose to partner with professionals to handle guest communication, cleaning, and maintenance. You can explore the best property management companies in Bulgaria for investors to ensure your property remains a top-rated listing throughout the year.

The Role of Property Condition

Guests on Airbnb have high expectations regarding cleanliness and modern amenities. If you have purchased an older property to secure a lower entry price, you may need to invest in upgrades. Before you begin, it is essential to research the nuances of renovating an old house in Bulgaria: costs and practical tips to avoid common pitfalls that could inflate your budget and delay your entry into the rental market.

Is It Worth the Investment?

The short answer is yes—provided you conduct thorough due diligence. The Bulgarian Airbnb market rewards those who invest in premium locations, prioritize professional property management, and keep their units updated to modern standards. While the market is maturing, the combination of low taxes, affordable property prices, and a steady stream of international tourists makes Bulgaria a standout choice for real estate investors looking to diversify their portfolios.

However, investors should remain mindful of local regulations, including the registration of tourist accommodations with the municipal authorities. Keeping your business compliant is just as vital as securing a high-performing property.
