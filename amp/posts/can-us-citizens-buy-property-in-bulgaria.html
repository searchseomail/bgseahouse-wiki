Can US Citizens Buy Property in Bulgaria?

urban_wanderer
24.07.2026

Bulgaria has emerged as a premier destination for international investors seeking affordable luxury, stunning coastal landscapes, and historical charm. For many US citizens, the dream of owning a vacation home or a rental asset in Europe is becoming a reality in this Balkan nation. However, navigating the legal complexities of foreign property ownership requires careful planning and a clear understanding of local regulations.

Legal Requirements for US Citizens Buying Property in Bulgaria

The short answer is yes: US citizens can buy property in Bulgaria. However, there is a specific legal nuance regarding the nature of the property being purchased. Under the Bulgarian Constitution and the Law on Ownership, foreign citizens from non-EU countries—including the United States—can freely purchase apartments, condos, and houses without land.

If you intend to purchase a house that includes a plot of land, the process is slightly different. US citizens generally cannot own land in their personal capacity due to the lack of a reciprocal treaty between the US and Bulgaria regarding land ownership. To bypass this, most American investors register a Bulgarian limited liability company (EOOD or OOD). By purchasing the property through a company, the entity (which is a Bulgarian legal person) can legally own both the building and the land it sits on.

Steps to Purchasing Property

The buying process in Bulgaria is relatively streamlined, but it is essential to follow the correct legal steps to ensure your investment is secure. Before you commit to a purchase, it is highly recommended to research the current real estate market trends in Bulgaria to ensure you are paying a fair market price for your chosen location.

  • Property Search: Identify your target area, whether it is the vibrant Black Sea coast, the ski resorts of Bansko, or the historic capital, Sofia.
  • Due Diligence: Check the property for any existing mortgages, tax liens, or legal disputes.
  • Preliminary Contract: This document outlines the terms of the sale and usually requires a deposit of 10%.
  • Notary Deed: The final transfer of ownership is executed in front of a Bulgarian notary public.

Do You Need Legal Representation?

Many first-time buyers wonder, do I need a lawyer to buy property in Bulgaria? While it is not strictly mandatory by law to have an attorney, it is highly advisable. A qualified lawyer will conduct a thorough title search, verify the seller's documentation, and protect your interests during the signing of the preliminary contract.

Taxation and Ownership Costs

Owning property in Bulgaria comes with specific fiscal responsibilities. Once you have acquired your property, you will need to register it with the local municipality and the tax authorities within two months of the purchase. Below is a summary of the typical costs involved:

Cost Category Estimated Percentage/Fee
Notary Fees 0.1% to 1.5% of the property value
Local Tax 0.1% to 3% (varies by municipality)
Registration Fee 0.1% of the property value
Legal/Agency Fees 1% to 3% (if using professional services)

Generating Income from Your Bulgarian Property

Many US investors purchase property in Bulgaria as a buy-to-let investment. With the rise of tourism in areas like Varna, Burgas, and Sunny Beach, rental yields can be quite attractive. If you decide to go down this route, it is vital to understand the compliance side of the business. You should familiarize yourself with the regulations regarding how to rent out your Bulgarian property legally to avoid potential fines and ensure your income is reported correctly to the Bulgarian National Revenue Agency.

Conclusion

Buying property in Bulgaria as a US citizen is a straightforward process provided you utilize the correct legal structure for land ownership and conduct proper due diligence. By working with local experts and staying informed about market conditions, you can successfully diversify your portfolio with a high-value asset in one of Europe’s most affordable and beautiful countries.
