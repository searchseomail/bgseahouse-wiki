Buying a New Build Apartment in Bulgaria: What You Need to Know

midnight_tide
09.07.2026

Bulgaria has emerged as one of Europe’s most attractive real estate markets, offering a unique blend of affordability, stunning natural landscapes, and a growing economy. Whether you are looking for a holiday home on the Black Sea coast or an urban investment in Sofia, purchasing a new build apartment is often the preferred route for international investors and locals alike. However, navigating the Bulgarian property market requires a clear understanding of legal processes, construction standards, and local nuances.

Why Choose a New Build Apartment in Bulgaria?

New construction projects in Bulgaria offer distinct advantages over older properties. Modern buildings are constructed in compliance with contemporary EU energy efficiency standards, featuring better insulation, modern heating systems, and advanced plumbing. Beyond the physical structure, new developments often come with modern amenities such as underground parking, security, and landscaped gardens.

If you are exploring the broader market, you may want to research the best places to buy real estate in Bulgaria to determine whether a city center apartment or a coastal resort property aligns better with your long-term goals.

The Legal Stages of Purchasing

Buying a new build in Bulgaria typically follows a structured path involving several legal milestones. It is crucial to have a qualified local lawyer by your side to verify the developer's documents.

  • Preliminary Contract (Predvaritelen dogovor): This document outlines the terms of the sale, payment schedule, and completion deadlines. You will usually pay a deposit (typically 10-20%) at this stage.
  • Act 14: This is a critical milestone confirming that the building's structural frame is complete.
  • Act 15: This document confirms that the building is structurally sound and ready for final finishing works.
  • Act 16 (Permission for Use): This is the final and most important document. It certifies that the building meets all safety and regulatory standards and is officially ready for occupancy.

Understanding Maintenance Fees and Gated Communities

Many new build apartments are located within "gated complexes" (затворени комплекси). These developments offer managed security, communal pools, and fitness centers. However, these services come with mandatory annual maintenance fees. Essential Due Diligence

While new builds are generally safer, you should never skip the due diligence phase. Just as you would investigate what to check before buying rural property in Bulgaria, you must scrutinize the developer's portfolio. Ask for:

  • History of past projects (check if they were delivered on time).
  • Proof of ownership of the land plot.
  • Building permits and architectural plans.
  • Financial stability of the construction company.

Final Considerations for Investors

Bulgaria offers diverse opportunities, from urban residential hubs to seasonal vacation spots. While this guide focuses on apartments, those interested in the mountain market should also investigate the top locations for buying a ski chalet in Bulgaria if they are seeking a high-yield rental property. Regardless of your choice, ensure all payments are made via bank transfer to maintain a clear financial trail, and always insist on a notary deed (Notarialen akt) to finalize the transfer of ownership once the building is completed and the Act 16 is issued.
