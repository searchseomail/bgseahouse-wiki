How to Get Residency in Bulgaria by Buying Property

copper_fox
09.06.2026

Bulgaria has emerged as a premier destination for expatriates seeking a high quality of life within the European Union at a fraction of the cost found in Western Europe. For many investors and lifestyle seekers, the primary question is whether purchasing real estate grants automatic residency. It is a common misconception that buying property alone is a direct "golden visa" path, but with the right strategy, property ownership serves as a powerful foundation for obtaining long-term residency.

Understanding the Residency Framework in Bulgaria

To clarify, Bulgaria does not offer a direct citizenship-by-investment program based solely on the purchase of a residential apartment. However, real estate ownership is a significant asset when applying for a Long-Term Residence Permit. To successfully navigate the legal requirements, it is essential to understand the initial steps of how to buy property in Bulgaria as a foreigner, as a valid Title Deed is often a prerequisite for proving your ties to the country.

Key Residency Categories for Property Owners

While the purchase itself doesn't trigger residency, it supports your application under specific legal categories:

  • Commercial Representation: This is one of the most common routes. By registering a commercial representative office of a foreign company in Bulgaria, you can apply for residency. Owning a property provides proof of accommodation, which is mandatory for the application.
  • Business Investment: If you invest a substantial amount (typically over 600,000 BGN) in a Bulgarian company that creates jobs, you may qualify for permanent residency.
  • Retirement/Pensioner Status: Retirees who can prove a steady income and have a place to live—such as a house purchased in the countryside—can apply for residency. Many find buying a house in Bulgarian villages: the pros and cons to be a vital part of their retirement planning due to the lower cost of living and slower pace of life.

Financial Considerations and Property Investment

When planning your move, you must evaluate your budget. While property prices remain competitive, liquidity is key. Some investors wonder about can foreigners get a mortgage in Bulgaria?—the answer is yes, though it often requires a higher down payment and proof of local income or significant assets within the country.

Step-by-Step Guide to Applying for Residency

Once you have secured your property, the administrative process begins at the Migration Directorate of the Ministry of Interior. Follow these steps to ensure a smooth application:

  • Secure Legal Representation: Bulgarian immigration law is complex. Hiring a local lawyer is highly recommended to handle paperwork and ensure compliance with current ordinances.
  • Obtain a Criminal Record Certificate: This must be apostilled in your home country and translated into Bulgarian by a certified translator.
  • Open a Bulgarian Bank Account: You will need to show proof of financial means to support yourself during your stay.
  • Proof of Address: Your Title Deed (Notary Act) will serve as the primary document confirming your residential address in Bulgaria.
  • Health Insurance: You must purchase private health insurance or contribute to the National Health Insurance Fund (NHIF) to be eligible for a permit.

Maintaining Your Status

Residency permits in Bulgaria are typically issued for one year and must be renewed annually. After five years of continuous legal residence, you may become eligible to apply for Permanent Residency. It is crucial to maintain your property ownership and ensure all utility bills and local taxes are paid on time, as these documents act as proof of your ongoing integration into Bulgarian society. By combining a strategic property purchase with a clear residency pathway, you can establish a stable and fulfilling life in one of Europe’s most hidden gems.
