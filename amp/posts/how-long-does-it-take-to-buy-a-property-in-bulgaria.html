How Long Does It Take to Buy a Property in Bulgaria?

midnight_tide
04.07.2026

The journey to property ownership in Bulgaria is often perceived as a complex bureaucratic maze, but with the right preparation, it is a streamlined process. Generally, the timeline from finding your dream home to receiving the keys ranges from four to eight weeks. However, this duration is highly dependent on whether you are paying in cash, seeking financing, or purchasing land as a non-EU citizen.

Phase 1: Searching and Due Diligence (2–4 Weeks)

The initial phase involves browsing listings and physically inspecting properties. Whether you are looking for a luxury apartment in Sofia or considering buying a house in Bulgarian villages, the due diligence process is critical. During this time, your lawyer must verify the property’s status by checking the Property Register for any encumbrances, mortgages, or pending legal claims.

Phase 2: The Preliminary Contract (1–2 Weeks)

Once you have selected a property, the buyer and seller sign a Preliminary Contract. This document outlines the terms of the sale, the purchase price, and the completion deadline. At this stage, the buyer typically pays a deposit—usually 10% of the total purchase price—which serves as a guarantee that the property is taken off the market.

Phase 3: Finalizing the Sale (2–4 Weeks)

The final transfer of ownership must be executed before a local Notary Public in the jurisdiction where the property is located. If you are wondering about the financial logistics, it is worth noting that can foreigners get a mortgage in Bulgaria? Yes, but the approval process can add several weeks to your timeline due to bank appraisal requirements and credit checks.

Factors Influencing the Timeline

  • Payment Method: Cash transactions are significantly faster than those involving bank loans.
  • Legal Status of the Seller: If the seller is a company or if the property is part of an inheritance, the paperwork may take longer to verify.
  • Type of Property: Commercial properties or land plots often require additional documentation compared to standard residential apartments.
  • Seasonal Delays: August and late December are traditional holiday periods in Bulgaria, during which public offices and law firms may operate with reduced staff.

Summary of the Buying Process

Step Estimated Time
Property Search & Due Diligence 2–4 Weeks
Preliminary Contract & Deposit 1 Week
Mortgage Approval (if applicable) 3–6 Weeks
Notary Completion & Title Transfer 1–2 Days

While the process is relatively straightforward, buyers should always perform a cost-benefit analysis. Many investors are tempted by low entry prices, but it is important to research whether a cheap property in Bulgaria: is it really worth it? in the long run, considering renovation costs and potential rental yields.

Post-Completion Formalities

Once the Notary signs the Title Deed, the process is technically complete, but there are administrative "after-care" steps. Within 7 days of the purchase, you must register the property with the local tax office (Municipality) and the Bulstat Register. This is a mandatory step that ensures your tax records are accurate and that you are officially recognized as the owner in the national database.
